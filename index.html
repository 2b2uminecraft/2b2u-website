2builders2users

2b2u.net is a popular, modern Minecraft vanilla anarchy server. The map has been online for 5 years, preserving every player build and history.

Modifications & Mechanics

  • World Generation: Uses a modified vanilla generator to maintain map integrity.
  • Monsters: Reworked spawning logic to increase density near players.
  • Player Spawning: Spawns occur within 2048 blocks of 0,0.
  • Anticheat: Generally open, but blocks harmful movement exploits.
  • Item Duplication: Patched to prevent instability.

Terms of Service

The server hosts the survival gamemode as provided by Mojang, an open ended sandbox, in which there are no inherent rules related to gameplay elements like PvP and griefing. All players must follow the Minecraft Usage Guidelines, the Minecraft Community Standards, and the Xbox Community Standards. Do not engage in any personal or real-life harassment, threats or bullying. Reports should be sent to support@2b2t.org.

Server Issues

High latency and queue times are a natural part of the anarchy experience.

Useful Commands

  • /kill - Kill yourself when stuck in a bad spawn location.
  • /tpa <player> - Teleport to any player in the server as long as they accept it.
  • /deathmessagestoggle - Toggles the death messages.
  • /sethome /home /delhome - Manage your homes.

About

The world is 5 years old, with a size exceeding 300 GB and over 7,000 unique player joins. Everything you see on 2b2u is the result of the players themselves.
