Modifications & Mechanics

In our pursuit of a true anarchy experience, we permit the use of all client-side modifications. Players are free to customize their gameplay experience to best suit their survival and combat needs. While we maintain a vanilla-first philosophy, some adjustments are required to ensure the server remains stable and playable for all.

  • World Generation: We utilize a custom, modified vanilla terrain generator that preserves the aesthetic integrity of Minecraft while ensuring high-quality, diverse biomes.
  • Hostile Entities: Monster spawning logic has been refined to increase mob density near active player locations, heightening the difficulty of survival.
  • Spawn Dynamics: To prevent overcrowding and promote exploration, new players are spawned at random coordinates within a 200-block radius of the origin (0,0).
  • Combat & PvP: To ensure privacy and tactical secrecy, health bars, inventory status, and damage indicators are strictly hidden from other players.
  • Nether & Void Restrictions: For technical stability, movement on the Nether roof and traversal into the Void is prohibited; players attempting to access these areas will incur persistent damage until they return to normal space.

Terms of Service

By connecting to 2b2u.net, you acknowledge that you are entering a raw, unregulated survival environment. The server operates as a sandbox where griefing, raiding, and combat are not just allowed—they are inherent to the experience. We do not provide "safe spaces" or administrative intervention for player-to-player conflicts.

However, our commitment to an anarchy experience does not permit real-world harassment or the violation of fundamental platform standards. All players are bound by the following:

Community Guidelines

  • System Integrity: Do not use exploits, bots, or automated software designed to crash the server, induce artificial lag, or disrupt the connection for others.
  • Community Conduct: While gameplay is lawless, real-life harassment, personal threats, or doxxing will not be tolerated in-game or in external community channels.

Enforcement: The administration reserves the right to remove access to the chat or the server permanently if these community standards are breached. Evidence of misconduct should be submitted via email to support@2b2u.net.

Server Issues & Reporting

This dashboard serves as the central hub for monitoring known bugs, technical limitations, and ongoing server maintenance for the current 1.21.11 engine. As this is an anarchy server, periods of high latency are considered part of the natural load cycle.

  • Nether Roof Access: Resolved. Unauthorized players on the Nether ceiling are automatically relocated to the spawn region.
  • Chunk Loading: Optimization for chunk loading performance is currently a high-priority work-in-progress to minimize lag.
  • International Connectivity: Stability issues affecting players connecting from regions outside of North America and Europe have been patched.

Technical Support: If you encounter a game-breaking bug, please provide a detailed explanation and any relevant proof (screenshots or video) to bugs@2b2u.net. Note: Before submitting, please attempt to reproduce the bug on an entirely unmodified, vanilla 1.21.4 installation to ensure your client-side mods are not the source of the issue.

Essential Commands

The following commands are provided to assist in player navigation and emergency situations within the anarchy landscape. Please use them responsibly.

  • /kill: Use this if you are trapped in an unbreakable wall, void-locked, or in an otherwise inescapable death trap.
  • /tpa <player>: Request to teleport to another player. This requires the target player to explicitly accept the request.
  • /deathmessagestoggle: A personal setting to enable or disable public death notifications.
  • /sethome Set your home
  • /home Teleport to your home
  • /delhome Delete your home

About the History

2b2u.net stands as a testament to player-driven history. Over the past five years, our world file has grown to over 300 GB, capturing the remnants of thousands of player projects, massive infrastructure, and devastating wars. With over 7,000 unique players, the map reflects a living, breathing, and constantly decaying history.

There is no "reset" button here. The environment is entirely persistent; every block broken, every base razed, and every city built is a permanent feature of the world. By playing on 2b2u, you are not just a player; you are an author of the server's future history.
