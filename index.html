Modifications & Mechanics

Because 2b2u is a no-rules environment, all client-side modifications are permitted. We do not restrict your choice of software.

  • World Generation: Uses a modified vanilla generator to maintain map integrity.
  • Monsters: Reworked spawning logic to increase density near players.
  • Player Spawning: Spawns occur within 2048 blocks of 0,0 for map variety.
  • Anticheat: Generally open, but blocks harmful movement exploits.

Terms of Service

The server hosts the survival gamemode as provided by Mojang, an open-ended sandbox with no inherent rules regarding PvP or griefing. Players must follow Minecraft Usage Guidelines, Community Standards, and Xbox Community Standards. Do not engage in real-life harassment or bullying. Reports: support@2b2t.org.

Server Issues & Reporting

This page is for tracking any current issues, bugs, or limitations on the server with the current version of 1.21.4.

  • Nether Roof: Fixed. Those on the roof will be teleported to spawn.
  • Chunk Loading delays: In Progress.
  • Connection issues (Outside NA & EU): Fixed.

Reporting: Send an email to bugs@2b2t.org or create a submission on the 2b2t subreddit. Please be as detailed as possible. Before reporting, test if you can reproduce the issue with an unmodified 1.21.4 installation to rule out client modifications.

Useful Commands

  • /kill - Kill yourself when stuck.
  • /tpa <player> - Teleport to another player.
  • /sethome /home /delhome - Manage your homes.
