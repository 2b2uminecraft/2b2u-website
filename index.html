2builders2users

2b2u.net is a popular, modern Minecraft vanilla anarchy server. The map has been online for 5 years, preserving every player build and history.

Modifications & Mechanics

  • World Generation: Uses a modified vanilla generator.
  • Monsters: Reworked spawning logic.
  • Anticheat: Generally open, but blocks movement exploits.

Terms of Service

The server hosts the survival gamemode as provided by Mojang, an open-ended sandbox with no inherent rules regarding PvP or griefing. Players must follow the Minecraft Usage Guidelines and Community Standards. Do not engage in real-life harassment or bullying. Reports: bugs@2b2t.org.

Server Issues

Tracking issues for version 1.21.4. Before reporting, test with an unmodified 1.21.4 installation.

  • Nether Roof: Fixed.
  • Chunk Loading delays: In Progress.
  • Connection issues: Fixed.

Useful Commands

  • /kill - Kill yourself when stuck.
  • /tpa <player> - Teleport to another player.
  • /deathmessagestoggle - Toggle death messages.
  • /sethome /home /delhome - Manage homes.
