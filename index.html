Modifications & Clients

2b2u operates without an anti-cheat system. Because the server is entirely unmoderated, the use of hacked clients—including but not limited to Impact, Future, RusherHack, and Kami Blue—is not only permitted but encouraged for survival.

There are no bans for "illegal" items, exploits, or duping. You are responsible for your own defensive capabilities.

Infrastructure & Issues

High latency (ping), TPS (ticks per second) fluctuations, and login queues are a fundamental feature of the server's infrastructure. Due to the anarchy nature of the gameplay, administrative intervention is strictly limited to server-critical hardware failures.

If you encounter a "Connection Lost" error, it is likely a result of server load or a player-triggered exploit. Reconnecting is the standard solution.

Server Commands

The command list is minimal to preserve the vanilla experience:

  • /queue — View your position in the priority or standard login queue.
  • /stats — View your player metrics, including join date and total deaths.
  • /kill — Instant suicide to prevent capture or respawn at base.

About the Server

2builders2users (2b2u) was founded on the principles of absolute player freedom. Since its inception, the world map has never been reset, resulting in a world-file size that documents every base ever built, every road ever paved, and every monument ever destroyed.

The server has no rules, no staff, and no censorship. Everything you see, build, or destroy is the result of the players themselves.
